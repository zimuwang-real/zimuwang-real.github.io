Areas

How the collection is organized

The projects build from direct pixel-space operations to geometry-aware models and then to learned representations.

Image reconstruction and filtering

  • Recover color from stacked monochrome scans by aligning channels.
  • Use convolution and frequency reasoning for edges, sharpening, and hybrids.
  • Blend images across scales with Gaussian and Laplacian pyramids.

Correspondence and projective geometry

  • Estimate homographies from point matches.
  • Use Harris corners, feature descriptors, and RANSAC to reject outliers.
  • Warp overlapping photos into a shared panorama.

Learned scene and image models

  • Calibrate cameras and train a NeRF for novel-view synthesis.
  • Use a pretrained diffusion model for denoising, editing, and inpainting.
  • Train time-conditioned and class-conditioned UNets from scratch.
