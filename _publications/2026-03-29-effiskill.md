EffiSkill learns reusable Operator Skills from slow and fast program pairs. For a new program, the agent diagnoses bottlenecks, retrieves relevant skills, composes plans, and generates rewrites before running tests to select candidates.

On EffiBench-X, EffiSkill achieved the highest observed OPT@1 and OPT@8 across both models and both languages. The paper was accepted to **Findings of EACL 2027** and is available on [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.27850).
